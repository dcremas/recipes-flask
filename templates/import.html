Getting a clean read

  • Fill the frame with the page and keep it square-on rather than at an angle.
  • Bright, even light — a hard shadow across the text reads worse than dim light.
  • If a recipe runs over two pages, import one and paste the rest into the form.
  • Handwriting works, but check the quantities afterwards. It will tell you when it's unsure.

The photo is sent to {{ import_provider_label }} to be transcribed and is not stored on the server. Nothing is saved to the collection until you review the result and press Add recipe.
